# Health and safety

Prompts that support, never replace, competent health and safety management. Every output must be reviewed by a competent person.

## RAMS review

**Use it for:** a first-pass review of a risk assessment and method statement before formal review.

~~~
You are an experienced construction health and safety adviser working to UK practice [or UAE practice and local authority requirements]. Review the RAMS below for [activity].

Report:
1. Hazards that appear to be missing for this activity
2. Control measures that look weak or vague
3. Sequencing issues in the method statement
4. Missing information: permits, plant certification, competence, emergency arrangements
5. Questions for the subcontractor before approval

RAMS: [paste]
~~~

**Check before you use it:**
- A competent person carries out the formal review and approval
- Site-specific conditions are checked on site, not assumed

## Toolbox talk

**Use it for:** preparing a short, practical toolbox talk.

~~~
You are a site safety lead. Write a five-minute toolbox talk on [topic, eg working at height on mobile towers] for [audience, eg drylining operatives].

Include: why it matters, three to five key rules, common mistakes seen on site, what to do if something goes wrong and three questions to check understanding. Plain language. Under 400 words.
~~~

**Check before you use it:**
- Rules match your site rules and the manufacturer's guidance

## Incident summary

**Use it for:** structuring an initial incident summary for internal reporting.

~~~
You are a health and safety manager. From the notes below, produce an initial incident summary.

Sections: what happened (facts only), date, time and location, people involved (roles, not names), immediate actions taken, potential causes to investigate, reporting obligations to check, next steps.

Do not assign blame or speculate beyond the notes.

Notes: [paste]
~~~

**Check before you use it:**
- Statutory reporting duties (for example RIDDOR in the UK) are checked by a competent person
- Personal data is handled under your firm's policy
