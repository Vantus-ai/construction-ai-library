# Construction AI Library

An open library of AI prompts and Claude skills for construction and built environment workflows. Written by practitioners, for practitioners.

Most firms in our industry now have access to general-purpose AI. Far fewer have changed a single workflow with it. This library closes part of that gap: tested, copy-ready prompts and installable skills for the documents and decisions construction teams deal with every week.

Maintained by [Vantus AI](https://vantusai.co.uk), AI implementation for the built environment.

## How to use it

1. Pick the workflow you do most often.
2. Copy the prompt, fill in the square brackets and paste it into your AI tool.
3. Run the checks listed under each prompt before the output leaves your desk.
4. Once a prompt works for you, save your own version. That is the start of a team library.

## Ground rules

- **Protect your data.** Remove client names, addresses, commercially sensitive figures and personal data unless your firm has approved the tool for that data.
- **Check the contract.** AI gets clause numbers and time bars wrong. Always verify against the actual contract and any amendments.
- **You stay accountable.** These prompts produce drafts. Professional judgement, sign-off and responsibility stay with you.
- **Not advice.** Nothing here is legal, contractual or professional advice.
