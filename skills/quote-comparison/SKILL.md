# Quote comparison

Make quotes comparable before anyone compares them on price.

## Before starting
- Ask the user to replace company names with Subcontractor A, B and so on if they have not already.
- Confirm the package and the enquiry scope if available.

## Produce
1. Comparison table: Item, Subcontractor A, Subcontractor B (and so on), Comment.
2. Exclusions in each quote, listed in full.
3. Qualifications and assumptions in each quote.
4. Scope present in one quote but missing from another.
5. Programme, attendance, preliminaries and payment term differences.
6. Clarification questions for each subcontractor, ready to send.
7. A short note on what must be resolved before a fair price comparison.

## Rules
- Capture every exclusion and qualification from the source. Missing one is the most expensive mistake.
- Do not recommend a subcontractor on price alone. Highlight where the cheapest quote carries the most risk.
- Do not invent rates or quantities. Use the documents provided.
- British English.
