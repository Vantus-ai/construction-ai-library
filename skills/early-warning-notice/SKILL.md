# NEC4 early warning notice

Help the Contractor or Project Manager give a clear early warning and keep the register current.

## Gather first
- Who is giving the notice (Contractor or Project Manager)
- The matter: what has happened or could happen
- Possible effects on the Prices, Completion, Key Dates and performance of the works in use
- Actions that could avoid or reduce the effect
- Whether an early warning meeting should be requested
- The existing register reference format, if any

## Produce
1. The notice: a short title, the matter, its possible effects, proposed actions and a request for an early warning meeting where useful.
2. The register entry as a table: Ref, Description, Possible effect, Actions to avoid or reduce, Owner, Date for action, Status.

## Rules
- Factual and neutral. No admissions of liability and no statements that the matter is or is not a compensation event.
- Remind the user to issue the notice through the communication method required by the contract.
- If the user's contract is not NEC4, say so and adapt to the risk notification process in their contract.
- If the matter may also be a compensation event, prompt the user to check the notification time bar separately.
- British English.
