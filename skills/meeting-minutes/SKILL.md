# Meeting minutes

Turn raw notes or a transcript into minutes people actually read.

## Gather first
- Meeting type and number
- Date, attendees and organisations
- Agenda, if there was one

## Produce
1. Summary of decisions made.
2. Minutes by agenda item: short, factual, past tense.
3. Action log table: Ref, Action, Owner, Due date, Status.
4. Items carried forward to the next meeting.
5. Date of next meeting if mentioned.

## Rules
- Never invent decisions, owners or dates. Mark missing owners or dates as TBC.
- Record contentious points no more firmly than they were agreed. Where views differed, say so neutrally.
- Use roles or initials consistently.
- Carry forward open actions from previous minutes if the user provides them.
- British English.
