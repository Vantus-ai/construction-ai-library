# Site diary summary

Turn daily site records into a weekly report without losing anything that matters later.

## Produce
1. Progress by area or trade.
2. Labour and plant levels, with shortfalls against plan if known.
3. Weather, access or utility issues and their effect.
4. Instructions received: date, from whom and what.
5. Health and safety observations.
6. Issues that may lead to delay or additional cost, with dates.
7. A short look-ahead if the records include one.

## Rules
- Keep dates, names, times and instruction sources exactly as recorded. These may support or defend a claim later.
- Never summarise away a delay, instruction or disruption, however small.
- Do not add causes or opinions that are not in the records.
- Flag gaps in the records, such as missing days or missing labour returns.
- Under 400 words unless the user asks for more. British English.
